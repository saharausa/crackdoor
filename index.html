The reset

Change the inputs.

Fix the stale stuff. Keep what works. Move.

Stage 01 · Audit

Audit it.

+

Look at the profile without your own bias.

Identify the strongest first impression.
Find the photos or prompts creating drag.
Clarify what kind of match the profile is signaling for.
Stage 02 · Reorder

Reorder it.

+

Use what you have before shooting anything new.

Lead with the strongest photo.
Remove repetition and mixed signals.
Make the changes and stop over-editing.
Stage 03 · Capture

Fill the gaps.

+

Only shoot what the profile still needs.

Know which photo types you actually need.
Capture range: face, full body, social, activity, environment.
Use natural photos that still look like you.
Stage 04 · Re-enter

Go.

+

Ship the profile. Use the apps. Go meet people.

Stop treating every swipe as a verdict.
Use social settings and local networks too.
Only change the profile again when there is a reason.
